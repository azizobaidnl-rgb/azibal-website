WHO WE WORK WITH

Professional supplier relationships.

We are interested in speaking with established brands, manufacturers, authorised distributors and other legitimate suppliers that can provide relevant product ranges and clear wholesale terms.

01

Brands

Consumer brands looking for suitable wholesale distribution or retail opportunities can introduce their range for review.

02

Manufacturers

Manufacturers with suitable products and reliable supply capacity are welcome to discuss potential purchasing relationships.

03

Authorised Suppliers

We welcome legitimate distributors and wholesale suppliers with clear product sourcing and commercial documentation.

WHAT SUPPLIERS CAN EXPECT

Clear communication from first contact.

01

Business Introduction

We review your company, product range, wholesale programme and relevant commercial information.

02

Product Discussion

We discuss categories, products, minimum order requirements, pricing structure and availability.

03

Channel Requirements

We discuss permitted sales channels, territories, marketplace restrictions and other brand requirements before proceeding.

04

Commercial Review

Where there is a suitable fit, both parties can review the relevant terms and next steps.

OUR APPROACH

We respect supplier requirements.

Every brand has different distribution rules. AziBal aims to work within agreed commercial terms rather than making assumptions about where or how products may be sold.

Marketplace Policies

Marketplace sales are considered according to the supplier's written policy and any agreed restrictions.

Territory Requirements

We discuss geographic distribution requirements before entering into a commercial relationship.

Pricing & Minimum Orders

Wholesale pricing, minimum order quantities and purchasing frequency are discussed directly with each supplier.

WHAT TO SEND

Help us review your proposal efficiently.

When contacting AziBal, suppliers can include the following information where available:

Company Information

Company name, website, country, business contact and a short introduction to your organisation.

Product Information

Brand names, categories, product catalogue or line sheet and the markets in which your products are available.

Wholesale Information

Wholesale pricing structure, MOQ, lead times, payment terms and any distributor or marketplace requirements.

BUSINESS INFORMATION

Transparent about where AziBal is today.

AziBal operates from the Netherlands and is currently developing its supplier network and wholesale operations. We do not claim certifications, supplier relationships or business registrations that have not been completed or verified.

As the business develops, relevant registration and commercial documentation can be shared with suppliers during the approval process.
